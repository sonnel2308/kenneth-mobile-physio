Our Story

At Kenneth Mobile Physiotherapy, we believe that quality healthcare shouldn't require you to leave the comfort of your home. What started as a simple idea—bringing expert physiotherapy directly to those who need it most—has grown into a trusted service for patients across the region.

Our mission has always been to make physiotherapy accessible, convenient, and effective. We understand that mobility challenges can impact every aspect of daily life, and we're committed to helping you regain independence and confidence in your own space.

Whether you're recovering from surgery, managing chronic pain, or looking to improve your overall mobility, our dedicated team is here to support you every step of the way.

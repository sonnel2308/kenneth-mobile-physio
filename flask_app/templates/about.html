About Kenneth Mobile Physiotherapy

Expert, compassionate physiotherapy that comes to you

Our Mission

We are dedicated to providing accessible, high-quality physiotherapy services that help people of all ages maintain their mobility, independence, and quality of life. Our mobile service brings expert care directly to your home, hospital, or care facility, removing barriers to treatment and making rehabilitation accessible to everyone.

Our Team

Our team consists of highly qualified and experienced physiotherapists who are passionate about helping you achieve your health and mobility goals. We believe in a person-centred approach, treating you as an individual with unique needs and goals.

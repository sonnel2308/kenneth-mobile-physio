Expert Care in Your Comfort Zone

At Kenneth Mobile Physiotherapy, we bring expert healthcare directly to you, eliminating the need to travel for treatment. Whether you're recovering from surgery, managing chronic pain, or looking to improve your overall mobility, our dedicated team is here to support you every step of the way. Our mobile physiotherapy services are designed to help you maintain your independence, strength, and quality of life from the comfort of your home.

Aged Care Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy can play an important role in helping you to maintain your mobility, independence and quality of life. Through personalised assessment and treatment in the comfort of your home, physiotherapy can help manage pain, improve strength and balance, reduce fall risk, and maintain your ability to perform everyday activities safely.

At Kenneth Mobile Physiotherapy, we provide practical, individualised rehabilitation programs tailored to your goals, abilities and home environment. We focus on helping you remain as independent, active, and confident as possible, while supporting you to continue living safely at home.

We work closely with case managers, families and support workers to ensure care is coordinated and aligned with your goals.

📰

Physiotherapy for NDIS Participants

Physiotherapy can support NDIS participants to improve their mobility, strength, independence and ability to participate in everyday activities.

At Kenneth Mobile Physiotherapy, we provide personalised in-home physiotherapy based on each participant's individual needs, goals and functional capacity.

Support may include:

  • Mobility and functional assessments
  • Strength, balance and falls prevention
  • Walking and transfer training
  • Pain management
  • Exercise programs tailored to individual abilities
  • Equipment and mobility advice
  • Strategies to improve independence with daily activities
  • Liaison with support coordinators, carers and other healthcare professionals where appropriate

Falls prevention and Balance Training

Physiotherapy can help older adults improve their balance, strength and confidence while reducing their risk of falls.

At Kenneth Mobile Physiotherapy, we provide personalised in-home assessment and exercise programs designed to help clients:

  • Improve balance, strength and mobility
  • Reduce falls risk
  • Walk and transfer more safely
  • Build confidence with daily activities
  • Maintain independence at home
  • Stay active and engaged in the community

Each program is tailored to the client's individual needs, abilities and goals, with a strong focus on safe mobility, independence and quality of life.

Physiotherapy After Joint Replacement

Physiotherapy plays an important role in helping you recover after joint replacement surgery. Kenneth Mobile Physiotherapy provides personalised in-home rehabilitation to help you regain movement, strength and confidence.

Treatment may include:

  • Improving joint movement and strength
  • Walking and mobility retraining
  • Managing pain and swelling
  • Balance and falls prevention
  • Building confidence with everyday activities
  • A tailored home exercise program

The goal is to help you recover safely, return to your daily activities and regain your independence.

🦍

Chronic Condition and Pain Management

Physiotherapy can assist people living with chronic health conditions to maintain mobility, strength and independence while managing the physical challenges associated with their condition.

We also support people living with acute and persistent pain through hands-on treatment.

The aim is to help clients better manage their pain, improve function and maintain independence in everyday life.

Mobility Aids & Training

The right mobility aid can make everyday movement safer, easier and more comfortable.

Kenneth Mobile Physiotherapy can assess your mobility needs and provide advice on suitable aids, including:

  • Walking frames and walkers
  • Walking sticks
  • Crutches and other mobility equipment
  • Safe and correct use of mobility aids
  • Walking, transfers and mobility training

Your mobility and equipment needs are assessed and regularly reviewed to ensure your aid remains appropriate, safe and effective as your needs change.

Personalised guidance to help you move safely and confidently at home and in the community.

👩‍⚕️

Neurological Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy can help people living with neurological conditions such as stroke, Parkinson's disease and other neurological conditions maintain movement, strength and independence.

Treatment is tailored to each person and may focus on walking, balance, coordination, strength, transfers and falls prevention, as well as making everyday activities safer and easier. Supporting clients to move with greater confidence, stay active and maintain their independence.

Why Choose Mobile Physiotherapy?

Traditional physiotherapy often requires travel to a clinic, which can be challenging for those with mobility issues. Our mobile service brings the clinic to you, making recovery more accessible and convenient.

Our certified physiotherapists arrive fully equipped with all necessary tools and equipment to provide comprehensive care right in your home. We tailor each treatment plan to your unique needs, ensuring you receive the most effective care possible.

Whether you're recovering from an injury, managing a chronic condition, or looking to improve your overall fitness, our home-based approach allows you to recover in your own environment, which can significantly speed up the healing process.

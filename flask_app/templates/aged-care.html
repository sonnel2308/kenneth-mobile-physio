Aged Care Funding

Our aged care physiotherapy services support older adults maintain their independence and mobility. We work with residential aged care facilities, home care packages, and private aged care clients to deliver comprehensive rehabilitation and maintenance programs.

🏠

Home Care Packages

We provide physiotherapy services funded through Commonwealth Home Care Packages, helping older adults stay independent in their own homes while receiving professional care.

🏥

Residential Aged Care

Our team visits aged care facilities to provide on-site physiotherapy assessments, treatment, and rehabilitation programs tailored to residents' needs.

👵

Private Aged Care Clients

We accept private payment from aged care clients who prefer to use their own funds for physiotherapy services, offering flexible scheduling and personalized care plans.

📋

Assessment & Referrals

We can assess clients for aged care funding eligibility and assist with referrals to aged care providers and funding coordinators.

🤝

Why Choose Our Aged Care Services?

We understand the unique needs of older adults and work to preserve their mobility, strength, and independence. Our approach to aged care physiotherapy focuses on:

  • Personalized treatment plans that respect individual capabilities
  • Regular progress assessments and adjustments
  • Education for clients and families on maintaining mobility
  • Coordination with aged care providers for seamless care
  • Focus on fall prevention and safety

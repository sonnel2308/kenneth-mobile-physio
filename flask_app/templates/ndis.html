Our NDIS Services

As NDIS-registered providers, we deliver a comprehensive range of physiotherapy services including:

  • Assessment and goal setting
  • Manual therapy and joint mobilization
  • Strengthening and conditioning programs
  • Balance and fall prevention
  • Assistive device prescription and training
  • Home exercise programs
  • Education for participants and families

We work within the NDIS price guide framework and can provide detailed pricing information upon request.

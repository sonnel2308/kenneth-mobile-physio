DVA Gold and White Card

We provide DVA (Department of Veterans' Affairs) funded physiotherapy services to Australian Defence Force veterans, reservists, and family members who hold a Gold or White DVA Health Card.

⭐

Gold Card Holders

Gold card holders receive full DVA support for their healthcare needs. We can bill directly to DVA for eligible physiotherapy services covered under your DVA Gold Card.

⭐

White Card Holders

White card holders have access to DVA healthcare services with some co-payment requirements. We can assist with billing and provide information on your co-payment obligations.

🎖️

Veterans' Services

We understand the unique health needs of veterans and provide compassionate, respectful care with sensitivity to military service experiences.

🔗

Direct Billing

For eligible services, we can provide direct billing to DVA, simplifying the payment process for card holders.

🇦🇺

DVA Card Types and Benefits

Understanding your DVA card type is important for accessing the right support:

  • Gold Card: Full coverage for most healthcare services including physiotherapy
  • White Card: Coverage with co-payment for some services
  • Services available: Physiotherapy, allied health, medical appointments
  • Eligibility: Australian Defence Force veterans, reservists, and eligible family members

If you're not sure about your DVA card status or coverage, we can help you verify your eligibility and guide you through the process.

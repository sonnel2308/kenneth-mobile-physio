NDIS

Supporting your client's goals, independence and quality of life.

At Kenneth Physiotherapy, we provide personalised mobile physiotherapy for NDIS participants in the comfort of their own home, supported accommodation or community setting. We understand that every person is different. Our physiotherapy focuses on your individual goals, abilities and everyday needs, helping you improve your physical function and participate more confidently in the activities that are important to you.

Unlike many other providers, you won't be waiting days to hear back from us.

We work collaboratively with participants, families, support workers, support coordinators, plan managers and other healthcare professionals to ensure physiotherapy is practical, goal-focused and suited to the participant's individual circumstances.
